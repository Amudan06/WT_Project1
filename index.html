About the company

Threadloom Knits is a knitwear manufacturer based in Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu, India's knitwear capital. For 18 years we have turned cotton into quality garments for brands, retailers and corporates.

Every garment is made in-house, from yarn to finished product, so we control quality and delivery at each stage. With 3 factories and an output of 12 lakh garments a year, we handle both small trial orders and large repeat orders.

We are ISO certified, and our customers tell us the reasons they return are consistent quality, correct export documents and on-time delivery.

Why buyers choose us

  • Complete in-house process, spinning to packing
  • Orders from 200 pieces (sets) upward
  • Private label and custom branding
  • Export-ready documentation
  • Quality backed by ISO certification

How your garment is made

Five steps, all managed by our own teams.

  1. SpinningRaw cotton is spun into yarn.
  2. KnittingYarn is knitted into fabric on machines.
  3. DyeingFabric is dyed to your colours.
  4. Cutting and stitchingFabric is cut and stitched into garments.
  5. PackingGarments are packed and dispatched.
